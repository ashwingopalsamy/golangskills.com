# Go: Fintech

This collection owns monetary and payment-domain integrity rather than generic payment API integration.

It contains:

- `go-money-and-ledgers`
- `go-payment-lifecycles`
- `go-financial-idempotency`
- `go-clearing-settlement-reconciliation`
- `go-fintech-security-compliance`
- `review-go-fintech-change`

Its zero-tolerance failures are silent precision loss, unbalanced committed journals, duplicate money movement, invented payment success, missing reconciliation evidence, and sensitive authentication-data leakage.

Provider, rail, scheme, currency, jurisdiction, and compliance facts remain version- and context-specific. These skills require current primary evidence and explicitly do not provide legal advice or compliance certification.
