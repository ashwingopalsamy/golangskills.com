# Naming decision

The public family is **Go: Production Engineering**, **Go: Distributed Systems**, and **Go: Fintech**. Their stable collection IDs remain `engineering-skills-for-go`, `distributed-systems-skills-for-go`, and `fintech-skills-for-go`; display-name changes do not break package or installation identity.

The `Go: <Domain>` namespace groups the three listings visually and keeps the language term first for human and directory discovery. Production Engineering owns general implementation and lifecycle work. Distributed Systems and Fintech remain specialist collections with precedence when their failure or integrity invariants dominate.

`golangskills.com` remains the repository and future discovery-site identity. `Go` is used in public names; `Golang` remains an accurate search synonym in metadata rather than the product name.
