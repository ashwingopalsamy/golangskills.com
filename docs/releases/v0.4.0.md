# Go plugin family v0.4.0

Initial coordinated release of the three Go skill collections from one canonical source.

## What shipped

- Go: Production Engineering: APIs, services, testing, performance, security, operations, and review.
- Go: Distributed Systems: concurrency, consistency, messaging, resilience, coordination, and partial failure.
- Go: Fintech: money, ledgers, payments, idempotency, settlement, reconciliation, and compliance.
- Stable collection and skill IDs.
- Collection-specific CC0 Gopher artwork, capabilities, starter prompts, and generated packages for Codex/ChatGPT, Claude Code, Cursor, and OpenCode.
- More precise mixed-domain routing cases and refreshed discovery catalogs.

## Design notes

Specialist precedence stays explicit: financial integrity goes to Fintech; cross-process consistency and partial failure go to Distributed Systems; general Go engineering goes to Production Engineering.

No category-leadership claim is made. The larger comparison campaign remains deferred until the release-candidate benchmark is ready.
