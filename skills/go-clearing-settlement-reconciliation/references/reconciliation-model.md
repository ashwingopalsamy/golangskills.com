# Reconciliation model

1. Acquire and authenticate source evidence.
2. Store immutable artifact and checksum.
3. Parse with a versioned deterministic parser.
4. Normalize identities, amounts, currencies, and lifecycle.
5. Match one-to-one under explicit rules.
6. Classify unmatched and mismatched items.
7. Age timing differences.
8. Authorize and post linked adjustments.
9. Re-run safely and preserve decisions.
10. Prove source and processing completeness.
